Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a quote?

From the Dashboard, tap the + button, pick or add a client, add your line items, and either save it as a draft or send it right away.

How do I get paid?

Once a quote is approved, convert it to an invoice. From there you can collect a card or Apple Pay payment (once you've connected a bank account under Settings → Payments), or mark it paid manually for cash, check, Zelle, or Venmo.

What's included in the free plan?

Everything — the free plan is capped by volume (3 quotes/invoices per month), not by features. Nothing you build on the free plan ever gets locked behind a paywall later.

How does JobBench Pro pricing work?

Pro is a flat monthly or annual subscription. Once you subscribe, your price is locked in for as long as you remain a customer — it never increases, even if we change pricing for new customers later.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Go to Settings → Subscription → Manage Subscription, which opens Apple's subscription management. You can also manage it directly in your iPhone's Settings app under your Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Can I export my data?

Yes — data export is always available, even on the free plan and after you cancel a subscription.
